Our Goal

No VSU student should have to struggle through a class alone.

Tutee makes it easy for VSU students to find help from peers who have already passed their courses, and gives strong students a way to give back and earn experience.

What we stand for

Access

Help should be easy to find. Search by course and book a session in minutes.

Community

Trojans helping Trojans. Tutors know the campus, the courses and the pressure.

Success

Better grades, less stress and more students staying on track to graduate.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Create an account with your VSU email.

  2. 2

    Pick your course

    Choose the class you need help with and browse tutors.

  3. 3

    Book a session

    Meet on campus or online at a time that works for you.
